Hassle-Free Execution

Our 4-Step Turnkey Process

How we take your space from initial measurement to stunning completion.

01

Consultation & Site Measurement

We conduct detailed site oculars, measure dimensions, discuss material preferences, and provide preliminary design quotes.

02

3D Layout & Off-Site Fabrication

Once layout is confirmed, modular cabinets and fixtures are precision-crafted in our workshop using heavy-duty marine plywood.

03

Demolition & On-Site Installation

Our skilled team handles old structure demolition, tile/slab works, electrical wiring, and seamless cabinet fitting.

04

Quality Check & Final Turnover

Thorough inspection of soft-closing hardware, joint seals, lighting tests, full site cleanup, and official client turnover.
